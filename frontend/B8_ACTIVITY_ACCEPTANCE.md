# Wider activity and launch balance acceptance

First apply migration/publication using [LAUNCH_ACTIVITY.md](../backend/LAUNCH_ACTIVITY.md).
On a Mac, run `bash frontend/scripts/test-mac.sh` (XCTest/UI and Release simulator
build), then install the updated app over the existing iPhone installation.
Preserve the existing app data, account and pending queue. HealthKit Background
Delivery entitlement must be supported by the signing profile.

1. Sign into the same account and reconnect. Existing step-only uploads must
   continue. Open Activity and enable optional quantities/workouts explicitly;
   review the Health permission sheet. The first readable daily totals and the
   workout enrolment timestamp establish baselines and award no old history.
2. Refresh storage after running the repeat-safe test-plant grant. Place the new
   Oak and Moonwillow sprouts with some clearance (centres 2,2 and 6,6 work).
   Check profile IDs `launch_oak` / `launch_moonwillow` in the API if comparing
   with old copies; growth-stage artwork alone cannot distinguish profiles.
3. Walk after enrolment, then Sync now: 1,000 newly eligible steps give each
   neutral placed test sprout **10 water**. Distance, active energy and flights
   should be readable independently; those quantities give these species no
   additional growth because their coefficients are omitted.
4. Record a supported workout through a tracker that writes to Health, after
   enabling workout uploads. Sync it from the tracker, then refresh this app.
   Ten eligible minutes give **2.5 extra water**, 60 give **15**, plus any new
   step water. Pauses/transitions do not count where interval detail exists.
   A placed adult Sleeping Dew source makes those values **2.6 / 15.6**.
5. Sync again and relaunch: no duplicate growth or milestone tokens. Turn off
   network during sync, reconnect and repeat: the saved request recovers once.
   Deny distance/energy/flights/workout reads independently; steps should remain
   usable. No readable data must be shown as unavailable rather than inferred
   zero or denied permission. Rejecting all step data can still allow workouts.
6. Manually add a workout in Health: it earns no workout bonus. Compare a real
   duplicate recording from two trackers: overlapping time is rewarded once.
   Test running/cycling segments in a multisport session and a session crossing
   account midnight; total eligible minutes must be conserved across days/types.
7. Visit Exchange: claim First movement once. Welcome the tutorial visitor;
   claim A first guest. At three total hosted visitors, claim A growing gathering.
   Those milestones total 120 tokens, plus earned visitor tokens, allowing a
   120-token Woodland pack before maturity. Buy/open one, verify disclosed odds
   and that the new copy is a stored sprout. Retry the claim/open after relaunch.
8. Grow to 100 / 400 / 1,000 cumulative water; confirm stage attraction changes.
   Check seedling/adult milestones are earned once by actual activity. Store and
   replant: stored copies stop growth/attraction, and retain progress/profile.
   Old pack openings and old placed plants keep their old behaviour.
9. Compare attraction near 2, 32 and a larger garden over enough time to avoid
   judging randomness from a few arrivals. At 32, base probability is 15.33% per
   eligible 30-second decision and offline rate is 4.6/hour; at 300, 60% and 18/hour.
   First opportunity and ten-decision gap protection affect short active samples.
   Leave and return: stored fractions/capacity and claim-once rewards persist.

Record device/OS, Mac build results, consent/queue recovery, workout source/types,
before/after growth and midnight boundaries. Windows syntax/resource checks are
not substitutes for these Apple-platform checks.
