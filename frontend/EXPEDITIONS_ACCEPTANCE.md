# Expedition native acceptance

Source implemented on Windows, 19 November 2026. Actual Xcode builds, hosted
XCTest/SwiftUI, RealityKit and physical HealthKit checks require a Mac/iPhone.
Portable source parsing/contract execution is not a substitute for these checks.

Run `bash frontend/scripts/test-mac.sh` for the project builds and native suite.
`ExpeditionTests` covers the HTTP-derived states, independent collection, quantity
copy, pinned pending claims and a sampled partition check of the actual SwiftUI
puzzle paths.

## Inspect in one batched device pass

- 320/375/393/430-point portrait widths, ordinary/XXXL/maximum accessibility text.
  Fifth bottom destination fits; Garden/camera stays mounted; navigation and Edit
  still work. Initial empty event, loading, unavailable and offline/cache states
  remain honest before any publication.
- All nine organic pieces share the same clipping/hit-test paths and a continuous
  painting. Test all edges/notches. Colour restores only after confirmed piece
  collection, not completion or milestone claims. Reentry does not replay reveals.
- Popup remains centred, with exact requirement and no separate quest heading.
  Quantity precedes token/gem/pack/item artwork. Every ratio is inside its bar,
  with two-line in-bar labels at accessibility sizes. Close and claim remain
  reachable while longer content scrolls. Full capacity/failure/pending messages
  are visible. Missing painting still exposes the exact quests and claims.
- VoiceOver has nine labelled piece controls and a protected popup focus scope;
  focus returns after closing. Close has a 44-point target. Colour alone conveys
  no state. Reduce Motion skips reveal motion; Reduce Transparency strengthens
  popup dimming. Inspect the stationary Basin comet/ripples and real-speed
  144-second Orrery loop, exact scales/clearances, dense-garden memory/frame rate.
- A month ends at 00:00 UTC for accounts with different saved game timezones.
  Page open at renewal fetches fresh progress; a pending old claim keeps its run
  ID. Existing arrangements may instantly requalify. Previous rewards/items and
  earned archive claims remain available; repeat rewards create new copies.
- Fresh Health enrollment mid-day, partial samples, denied/unreadable quantities,
  delayed reads, recorded runs/walks/pauses, device replacement and clock skew.
  Enrollment boundaries round up to millisecond wire precision; strict step sample
  interval filtering conservatively excludes samples spanning a cutoff.
- Three-week absence, 31-day month, first date almost 38 days old during grace.
  Observe bounded queries/uploads and independent step/workout coverage. Interrupt
  each read/chunk/upload and restart; UUID/body bytes resume unchanged. Ordinary
  growth remains seven days and historical evidence grants no water/XP/gems/tiers.
  Expired/prior-device evidence is not silently reissued with a new UUID.
- Store/re-place duplicate Basins/Orreries; +3% affects only steps. Orrery tiers
  give incremental 3/4/5 once per account game date, preserve progress while stored,
  never retroactively reward pre-placement steps or newly added plants, and remain
  separate from Morning Rain. Check shared Skills and preview parity.
- Switch account/server while a refresh, query, claim or catch-up is in flight.
  Old callbacks cannot populate the new account. Restore from the disk queue after
  a crash and after an uncertain response. Wallet and inventory update from the
  confirmed claim, including a later failed refresh.
